# Report layout (all four modes — 5-Section Table-Free Edition)

Every report uses the same **five sections in the exact same order**. **Sections 1–4 are 100% table-free** (using Single-Line Ticker Strips, Stacked 2-Line Mini-Cards, and Exception-Only Bullet Callouts for effortless reading on desktop and mobile). Compact reference tables are used **only in Section 5 (Appendix)**.

- **Section 1** is a **Comprehensive 1-Minute Executive Briefing** (`10–14` tight bullets, `<= 350 words`; `<= 400 words` in `WKND`) designed so Wilson gets the full market news, Street calls, book/execution snapshot, and top priorities even on days he cannot read the full document.
- **Section 2** is 100% focused on **market intelligence, top movers, Wall Street analyst upgrades/downgrades, and how those catalysts impact the underlying tickers you own** (zero option strikes, DTE, cushions, or Playbook rules).
- **Section 3** is 100% focused on **your active option/stock positions, strike cushions, Floor A/B math, execution grading, and Playbook v2.5 compliance**.

## Header (2 lines)
`<Report name> · <session date> · Ref <PM|MD|AH|WK>-<date>`
`Runner: <Claude|Gemini> · Updated HH:MM ET / HH:MM ICT (vN) · Feed: <feed name, run time> · Check: <check figure>`

## Section 1 — Comprehensive Executive Summary (10–14 bullets, ≤ 350 words; ≤ 400 words in WKND)
- `STALE FEED (<age>)` goes on the first line when the WarRoom PDF is older than the mode expects.
- **1. Macro, Rates & Vol Tape (`2–3 bullets`):**
  - Exact regular/futures/AH levels for `SPX`/`SPY`, `NDX`/`QQQ`, `RUT`/`IWM`, `SMH`, `XBI`, and `VIX`/`VIX1D`/`VXN`, plus the primary macro/geopolitical driver.
  - Yield curve (`US2Y`, `US10Y`, `US30Y`), `DXY`, oil (`WTI`/`Brent`), `Gold`, `BTC`, and Fed/FOMC rate pricing.
- **2. Top Market News, Earnings & Street Calls (`3–4 bullets`):**
  - Top 3–4 market-wide movers, BMO/AMC earnings surprises, and post-close SEC/corporate headlines with exact `%` moves and causes.
  - Key Wall Street analyst upgrades, downgrades, and PT changes, plus the direct fundamental read-through to your held tickers (`NVDA`, `MU`, `CRWV`, `CAPR`, etc.).
- **3. Book, Risk & Execution Snapshot (`2 bullets`):**
  - NAV, time-weighted return vs. `QQQ`, `R12` dry powder (`Cash + SGOV + STRC`) vs. the 15% floor, and active `R5` single-name / `BIO/SC` sleeve breaches.
  - Execution in one line (`X of Y actions done` + Cost of Indiscipline / realized P&L in `MID`/`AH`/`WKND`; carry-in actions and overnight fills in `PRE`; `S1–S5` stress check in `WKND`).
- **4. Top 3–5 Priorities & Session Gates (`3–5 bullets`):**
  - Ranked urgent orders with 🔴/🟡 markers, repeat triggers first (`REPEAT ×n` at count >= 3), plus the key `SPX`/`QQQ`/`VIX`/`US10Y` tripwire levels.

## Section 2 — Market intelligence & ticker read-through (Table-Free; Pure Market & Stock News)
**Boundary rule:** Section 2 covers macro, market-wide movers, primary catalysts, Wall Street analyst upgrades/downgrades (or timestamped lack thereof), 7-sleeve sector rotation, and **how those events affect the underlying stocks/tickers you own** (demand, margins, supply chain, peer sympathy, dilution, or clinical data). **Never include option strikes, DTE, delta, `%` strike cushions, `Floor A/B` strike tests, or Playbook v2.5 rules in Section 2.**

**Tell each story once.** A story (for example one company's earnings, or one macro release) is told in full in exactly one slot of Section 2. Other slots may name it in at most six words and point to that slot (`see 2.2 card 1`); they never restate its numbers. Index levels, sector prices and expected-move bands live in Appendix `A2`, not in Section 2 prose.

Slots, in order:
- **2.0 Market headlines (8–12 one-line items; the whole market, not only your names):**
  - One line each: `🔴/🟡/🟢 **<Category>:** <what happened, with the one or two numbers that matter> (<source>, <time>)`.
  - Categories to cover when there is news: macro data (actual vs consensus) · Fed and other central banks · Treasury auctions and rates · earnings results (revenue and EPS vs estimate, guidance, stock reaction) for the session's biggest reporters, held or not · deals, IPOs and M&A · policy, regulation and Washington · geopolitics and commodities · overseas markets · notable single-stock news.
  - No read-through line is required here; an item that touches a held name ends with `(held)` and is developed in 2.2.
  - At least half of the items must be about something other than your held names. An earnings item always carries the actual numbers; a mover always carries a sourced cause or `cause unconfirmed`.
- **2.1 Macro, rates, geopolitics & breadth (the driver, in 3 short paragraphs or strips):**
  1. **Primary driver:** what moved the tape and why (one story, told once).
  2. **Breadth & volatility:** whether breadth and vol confirm the move, in words, with at most 3 numbers per sentence. The full index, sector and band figures go to `A2`.
  3. **Rates, data & falsification trigger:** the yield-curve move, the day's economic releases vs consensus, Fed pricing, a one-line read-through to your held stock groups, and the explicit **falsification level** (`US2Y`, `US10Y`, `SPX`, `QQQ`, or `VIX`) that flips the session posture.
- **2.2 Top market movers, primary reasons & street upgrades/downgrades (Single-Line Ticker Strips + Deep-Dive Cards):**
  - **Top Market & Held-Ticker Movers (8–14 Single-Line Ticker Strips):**
    - Format each mover as one `·`-separated bullet:
      `🔴/🟡/🟢 **TICKER (Held / Non-Held)** $Price (+/-X.X%) · **Why:** <Verified primary catalyst from SEC filing, IR release, earnings print, or macro, else cause unconfirmed>` and then, **only when there is something real to say**, `· **Street:** <named analyst action>` and/or `· **Read-Through:** <effect on the business or on held sympathy tickers>`. Leave those two fields out when there is no analyst action or no read-through; never pad them.
    - A mover that has a Deep-Dive Card gets a short strip (`price · one-clause why · see card N`), not a second telling.
    - Cover (a) top market-wide gainers/losers and mega-cap leaders/laggards, (b) non-held sector bellwethers (`NVDA`, `AMD`, `TSM`, `AVGO`, `SMCI`, `QCOM`, `ARM`, `VST`, `CEG`, `SNDK`, `STX`, `LITE`), (c) BMO/AMC earnings movers, and (d) held underlyings moving >= +/-3%.
  - **Notable Street Upgrades, Downgrades & Initiations (0–8 Single-Line Strips; only real calls):**
    - Format each call as:
      `🔴/🟡/🟢 **Firm on TICKER (Held / Non-Held):** <Action & old -> new rating, old -> new PT> · **Thesis & Read-Through:** <1-line sell-side thesis and impact on sector or held tickers>.`
    - End with **one** sweep line listing the held movers that had `No public PT/rating change as of HH:MM ET`. That single line replaces a per-ticker "no change" note.
  - **Market & Filing Deep-Dive Cards (Top 3–5 Market/Ticker Catalysts):**
    - A card is the **only** full telling of its story. 3-bullet cards (`Filing & Print Forensics` · `Street Consensus & Analyst Reaction` · `Fundamental Read-Through to Your Tickers`) covering primary `8-K`, `10-Q` headline revenue/margin/cash/burn figures, `S-3`/`424B5` offerings, `Form 4` insider buys/sells, clinical trial endpoint/SAE data, or BMO/AMC earnings prints.
- **2.3 Seven-sleeve thematic & sector rotation:**
  - One bullet per sleeve, **direction and driver only, at most 30 words**, naming stories by reference rather than repeating their numbers (`AI Capex, Custom Silicon & Storage` · `Power/IPPs, Energy & Refiners` · `Enterprise Software & Cloud` · `Clinical Biotech & FDA` · `Rates, Fed & REITs` · `Crypto & High-Beta Proxies` · `China & Consumer`) showing sector direction, macro/micro driver, and fundamental impact on the underlying tickers you own (`WKND` adds % of NAV allocated and `Add / Hold / Trim / Freeze` posture).
- **2.4 Macro & market catalyst calendar (+ Screened Opportunities in PRE, AH, WKND):**
  - Upcoming macro releases (with consensus), Treasury auctions, Fed speakers, bellwether/held earnings dates, and (in `PRE`, `AH`, `WKND`) screened non-held `LC` (Mode A/B) and `BIO` (Mode A/B) watchlist candidates tagged `candidate — run LC/BIO underwriting first` (never staged as an order).

## Section 3 — Your active positions & book audit (Table-Free; 100% Portfolio, Contracts & Playbook v2.5)
**Boundary rule:** Section 3 never repeats general market stories from Section 2. It translates spot moves and SEC filings directly into your open option contracts, strike cushions, diluted cash-floor math, execution grades, and Playbook v2.5 compliance.

Slots, in order:
- **3.1 Active position alerts, strike cushions & Floor A/B math (Stacked 2-Line Mini-Cards):**
  - Format every triggered, moving (>= +/-3%), or defended position as a **Stacked 2-Line Mini-Card**:
    - **Line 1:** `🔴/🟡/🟢 **TICKER — <Contract / Shares> (<Spot $X.XX · Cushion +/-X.X% · Delta -0.XX · N DTE · Mark $X.XX>)**` (flagging any broken deep-ITM broker feed Greek as `bad Greek: true delta -1.00`).
    - **Line 2 (Indented):**
      - *For Biotechs (`BIO/SC`):* Exact diluted share count (`basic common shares + pre-funded warrants` from the latest `10-Q`/`S-3`), `Floor A` (`(Cash - Current Liabilities) / Diluted Shares`), `Floor B` (`Floor A - 2Q Burn`), `0.65 x Floor B` check vs. your strike, and the required Playbook v2.5 action (`R3`/`R4`).
      - *For Large Caps (`LC/MC`) & Short Stock:* Cushion vs. the `7%` / `Delta -0.30` `R4` trigger (or distance to the `+15%` `R11` short-stock stop), FCF/valuation support or peer sympathy impact from Section 2, and the required adjustment.
- **3.2 Execution scorecard & bleed audit:**
  - Execution grade (`X of Y actions done`), avoidable dollar bleed from missed covers/exits, audit of unplanned fills, and (in `WKND`) the monthly **Money Map** (top 3 profit and top 3 loss positions) + setup-tag strategy scorecard (`LC`, `BIO-A`, `BIO-B`, `BIO-C`, `WHEEL-CC`, `SHORT-STOCK`, `LOTTO`, `SCALP`).
- **3.3 Playbook v2.5 walkthrough (`R1`–`R12`) & exception-only R5 concentration check:**
  - Bullet walkthrough of `R1`–`R12` (`R12` dry powder >= 15%, `R3` <= 21 DTE ITM assignment wall vs. buying power, `R4` defense triggers & 1-ticket/day limit, `R2` velocity/50% profit-take & dead pennies, `R7` frozen underwater lots, `R10` wing/lotto limits, and `R11` short-stock stops & 2% cap).
  - **Exception-Only R5 Concentration Callouts:** Start with one summary bullet stating how many underlyings sit safely below the `8.0%` NAV amber threshold and the total `BIO/SC` sleeve % vs. the `25.0%` cap. Then list one `·`-separated bullet for every amber (>= 8.0% NAV), red (> 10.0% NAV), `hedge expires first`, or major dead-collateral (`R2`) position:
    `🟡/🔴 **TICKER (X.X% of NAV · $XX,XXX max loss):** <Paired / unpaired structure (`naked` / `spread width` / `hedged` / `hedge expires first`)> · **$XX,XXX gross collateral tied up**.`
- **3.4 Held-position binary implied-move vs. cushion radar (Single-Line Ticker Strips):**
  - One `·`-separated bullet per held position facing an earnings print, FDA/clinical readout, or macro binary within 14 days (doubling the underlying implied move `2x` for leveraged ETFs like `ASTX`, `CWVX`, `MSTU`, `SOLT`):
    `🔴/🟡/🟢 **TICKER (<Event & Date/Window>):** Implied move `+/-X.X%` (`2x` underlying if leveraged ETF) · **Open Exposure:** <Contracts> · **Cushion vs. Implied Move:** `+/-X.X%` cushion (`inside expected move` / `outside expected move`) -> **Action:** <Exact required action>.`

## Section 4 — Action plan (Table-Free; ≤ 500 words)
- **4.1 Orders (ranked, ≤ 8 Stacked 2-Line Mini-Cards; overflow to Appendix):**
  - Repeat triggers (`REPEAT ×n` at count >= 3) and working-order cancellations lead the list.
  - Format every order as a **Stacked 2-Line Mini-Card**:
    1. `🔴/🟡/🟢 **#<Rank> <STATUS / REPEAT ×n> — <Side & Action> <TICKER> <Expiry> <Strike><P/C> x <Qty> @ $<Limit> limit** (Mid $<X.XX> · Mark/Fill $<X.XX>)`
       - `**Feed row:** <ticker · type · strike · expiry · qty copied verbatim from WarRoom PDF> · **Rule:** <R# Name> · **Cushion:** <X.X%> · **Cash / Margin impact:** <$ impact> · **Why:** <1-line reason>.`
- **4.2 Do not touch:** 2–4 bullets for positions that cleared defense or should be held passively, and why.
- **4.3 Into the close / If-then gates:** 3–5 conditional bullets with exact levels on `SPX`, `QQQ`, `VIX`, `US10Y`, and held names.

## Section 5 — Appendix (Not counted in word caps; compact reference tables live here)
- **A1 Book (written FIRST, before any market search — the Book Lock, `SKILL.md` step 2):** feed line, check-figure line, `BOOK LOCK:` line, trigger scan line, full open short option table (`A4`), quoted & unquoted stock tables (`A3`), session fills (`A6`), and working-order audit (`A7`) (`WKND` includes the `S1–S5` stress table).
- **A2 Market data:** cross-asset dashboard table (including `2Y`/`10Y`/`30Y`, `2s10s`, `DXY`), 1σ/2σ daily & weekly expected-move bands, 11 GICS sectors table, Mag 7 table.
- **A3 Benchmark & scorecard** (AH summary line; WKND full `NAV History` and strategy scorecard tables).
- **A4 Journal rows** (AH/WKND).
- **A5 Playbook rule log, primary sources & Integrity line** (every number in Sections 1–4 logged).
- **A6 War Room Handoff** in a ```text code block.
