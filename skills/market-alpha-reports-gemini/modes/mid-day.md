# MID — Mid-Day Institutional Market Report (job: ADJUST)

Intraday US cash-session tape, breadth and volatility check, midday sector rotation, Wall Street analyst and filing updates, PRE-to-MID execution grading, and afternoon adjustment orders.
- **Horizon:** 09:30 ET open through 16:00 ET close.
- **Reads:** today's PRE report (`ACTIONS`, `TRIGGERS`), newest `MID` WarRoom PDF, live midday quotes.
- **Hands off to:** today's AH report.
- **Runs:** 09:30–15:59 ET.
- **Caps & Format:** Section 1 `<= 350 words` (Comprehensive Executive Briefing, `10–14` bullets) · Section 2 `<= 1,400 words` · Section 3 `<= 900 words` · Section 4 `<= 500 words` · **Zero tables in Sections 1–4** (tables only in Section 5 Appendix).

## What goes in each slot
- **Section 1 — Comprehensive Executive Summary (`10–14` bullets, `<= 350 words`):** (1) Intraday Cash Tape, Breadth, Rates & Vol Regime; (2) Top Intraday Movers, Morning Earnings & Street Calls + Held Read-Through; (3) Book Intraday NAV vs. QQQ, `R12` Dry Powder, `R5` Breaches & `X of Y PRE actions done`; (4) Top 3–5 Afternoon Orders & Into-the-Close Gates.
- **Section 2 — Market intelligence & ticker read-through:** `2.0 Market headlines (8–12 one-liners, whole market)` · `2.1 Intraday macro, rates, geopolitics & breadth` · `2.2 Top intraday movers, street upgrades/downgrades & SEC filings` · `2.3 Seven-sleeve thematic & sector rotation` · `2.4 Afternoon macro & earnings calendar`.
- **Section 3 — Your active positions & book audit:** `3.1 Intraday position alerts, strike cushions & Floor A/B math` · `3.2 PRE-to-MID execution scorecard & bleed audit` · `3.3 Playbook v2.5 walkthrough & exception-only R5 concentration check` · `3.4 Held-position binary implied-move vs. cushion radar`.
- **Section 4 — Action plan:** `4.1 Afternoon orders (ranked 2-line mini-cards with verbatim Feed row)` · `4.2 Do not touch` · `4.3 Into the close / If-then gates`.
- **Section 5 — Appendix:** `A1` book tables & intraday fills · `A2` cross-asset & 1σ/2σ bands · `A3` benchmark line · `A5` Playbook rule log, primary sources & Integrity line · `A6` War Room Handoff.
