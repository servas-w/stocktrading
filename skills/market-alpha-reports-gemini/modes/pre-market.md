# PRE — Pre-Market Alpha Report (job: PLAN)

Overnight global macro, US futures and pre-market tape recap, BMO earnings and overnight SEC filing sweeps, Playbook v2.5 pre-open book audit, carry-in actions, and opening-bell execution game plan.
- **Horizon:** overnight close through 09:30 ET cash open.
- **Reads:** latest AH/WKND report (`PUBLISHED CLOSES`, `ACTIONS`, `TRIGGERS`, `NAV:`), newest `PRE` or `EOD` WarRoom PDF, live pre-market quotes and BMO earnings releases.
- **Hands off to:** today's MID report.
- **Runs:** 04:00–09:29 ET.
- **Caps & Format:** Section 1 `<= 350 words` (Comprehensive Executive Briefing, `10–14` bullets) · Section 2 `<= 1,400 words` · Section 3 `<= 900 words` · Section 4 `<= 500 words` · **Zero tables in Sections 1–4** (tables only in Section 5 Appendix).

## What goes in each slot
- **Section 1 — Comprehensive Executive Summary (`10–14` bullets, `<= 350 words`):** (1) Overnight Global, Futures, Rates (`2Y`/`10Y`/`30Y`) & Vol Tape; (2) Top Pre-Market Movers, BMO Earnings & Morning Street Calls + Held Read-Through; (3) Book NAV, `R12` Dry Powder, `R5` Breaches & Carry-in Actions/Overnight Fills; (4) Top 3–5 Opening Orders & 09:30–09:45 ET Gates.
- **Section 2 — Market intelligence & ticker read-through:** `2.0 Market headlines (8–12 one-liners, whole market)` · `2.1 Overnight macro, futures, rates & vol` · `2.2 Pre-market movers, street upgrades/downgrades, BMO earnings & overnight SEC filings` · `2.3 Seven-sleeve thematic & sector rotation` · `2.4 Today's macro calendar & screened opportunities`.
- **Section 3 — Your active positions & book audit:** `3.1 Pre-open position alerts, strike cushions & Floor A/B math` · `3.2 Carry-in scorecard` · `3.3 Playbook v2.5 walkthrough & exception-only R5 concentration check` · `3.4 Held-position binary implied-move vs. cushion radar`.
- **Section 4 — Action plan:** `4.1 Opening orders (ranked 2-line mini-cards with verbatim Feed row)` · `4.2 Do not touch` · `4.3 Opening-bell & 09:45 ET gates`.
- **Section 5 — Appendix:** `A1` book tables · `A2` cross-asset & 1σ/2σ bands · `A3` benchmark line · `A5` Playbook rule log, primary sources & Integrity line · `A6` War Room Handoff.
