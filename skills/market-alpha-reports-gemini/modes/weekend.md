# WKND — Weekend Stock Market Report (job: STRATEGY & RISK)

Full weekly macro and market recap, weekly Street analyst and earnings synthesis, `NAV History` and `Trade Journal` weekly/monthly/since-Sep-28 performance & Money Map scorecard, `S1–S5` stress audit, and next week's strategic risk plan.
- **Horizon:** full week (Mon–Fri) plus next week's macro/earnings/catalyst calendar.
- **Reads:** the week's AH/MID/PRE reports, latest `WEEKBOOK` + week's `EOD` WarRoom PDFs, `Trade Journal` and `NAV History` sheets.
- **Hands off to:** Monday's PRE report (sets `PUBLISHED CLOSES` and `NAV:` lines).
- **Runs:** Fri 16:00 ET until Mon 04:00 ET.
- **Caps & Format:** Section 1 `<= 400 words` (Comprehensive Executive Briefing, `10–14` bullets) · Section 2 `<= 1,700 words` · Section 3 `<= 1,300 words` · Section 4 `<= 500 words` · **Zero tables in Sections 1–4** (tables only in Section 5 Appendix).

## What goes in each slot
- **Section 1 — Comprehensive Executive Summary (`10–14` bullets, `<= 400 words`):** (1) Weekly Macro, Rates, Curve & Vol Regime; (2) Top Weekly Market Themes, Major Earnings & Street Calls + Held Read-Through; (3) Weekly/MTD/Since-Sep-28 NAV vs. QQQ, Max Drawdown, Leverage, `S1–S5` Stress vs. 20% Cap, `R12`/`R5` Breaches & Weekly Execution Grade; (4) Top 3–5 Monday Opening Orders & Next-Week Risk Gates.
- **Section 2 — Market intelligence & ticker read-through:** `2.0 Market headlines (8–12 one-liners, whole market)` · `2.1 Weekly macro, rates, geopolitics & breadth` · `2.2 Weekly top movers, street upgrades/downgrades & SEC filings` · `2.3 Seven-sleeve thematic & sector rotation (with % NAV & Add/Hold/Trim/Freeze)` · `2.4 Next week's macro calendar & screened opportunities`.
- **Section 3 — Your active positions & book audit:** `3.1 Weekly position alerts, strike cushions & Floor A/B math` · `3.2 Weekly execution scorecard, Money Map & setup-tag strategy scorecard` · `3.3 Playbook v2.5 walkthrough & exception-only R5 concentration check` · `3.4 Next-week binary implied-move vs. cushion radar`.
- **Section 4 — Action plan:** `4.1 Monday opening orders (ranked 2-line mini-cards with verbatim Feed row)` · `4.2 Do not touch` · `4.3 Next-week If-then gates`.
- **Section 5 — Appendix:** `A1` book tables, weekly fills & `S1–S5` stress table · `A2` weekly cross-asset & 1σ/2σ bands · `A3` `NAV History` & strategy scorecard tables · `A4` Trade Journal rows appended · `A5` Playbook rule log, primary sources & Integrity line · `A6` War Room Handoff.
