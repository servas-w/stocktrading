- **Reads:** today's PRE and MID reports (ACTIONS, TRIGGERS, scenarios), the latest EOD WarRoom PDF (16:50–22:00 ET), live AH quotes and AMC earnings releases, `Trade Journal` and `NAV History` sheets.
- **Hands off to:** tomorrow's PRE (sets official `PUBLISHED CLOSES` and `NAV:` lines).
