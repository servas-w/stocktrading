# MID — Mid-Day Institutional Market Report (job: ADJUST)

What has moved since the open and why, whether the morning plan was carried out, and what to adjust for the afternoon.
- **Horizon:** only what happened since 09:30 ET; overnight context only as a labelled comparator.
- **Reads:** this morning's PRE (ACTIONS, TRIGGERS, scenarios), the latest AH/WKND Handoff (PUBLISHED CLOSES), the MID book source with working orders, the quote source.
- **Hands off to:** AH.
- **Runs:** 12:30 ET (00:30 ICT in US standard time, 23:30 ICT in daylight time).
- **Caps:** Section 1 ≤ 120 words · Section 2 ≤ 1,000 · Section 3 ≤ 400.

## Data checklist (after core steps 1–3)
- Book: MID feed + CHECK → full trigger scan; live marks on HARVEST/HOLD rows; working-order audit (opens on tested names, lottery tickets, stale GTCs); morning fills vs PRE ACTIONS.
- Market since the open: SPX/NDX/DJI/RUT, RSP vs SPY, 11 sectors, gap hold/fade, A/D (Source-or-N/A); > $50B leaders/laggards with volume pacing; Mag 7.
- Your names and their peers: moves since the open with cause.
- News since the open: 8-K, S-3/ATM, executive changes, analyst actions, FDA news on held names.
- Macro: reaction to the morning data; 13:00 ET auction; Fed remarks; yields, dollar, oil, gold, BTC.
- Tonight: AMC earnings of held names and peers with **expected move vs cushion**.

## What goes in each slot
**Section 1 — Summary:** market since the open · book (morning P&L direction, positions needing action) · `Execution: X of Y PRE actions done` + the biggest slip · top 3–5 items.

**Section 2 — Market & your book**
- **2.1 Story since the open:** the driver and whether the morning thesis held or broke (Tape grade with one-line reason).
- **2.2 Movers since the open:** your names, peers, market leaders and laggards; gaps that held vs faded.
- **2.3 Rotation:** which themes and sectors are winning and losing, one line each, with your exposure.
- **2.4 Your book midday:** positions that moved toward a trigger; harvest board (what to bank before the lunch lull); working orders to cancel (adds on tested names, R10 tickets); short-squeeze watch; intraday filings on held names.
- **2.5 What to watch this afternoon:** 13:00 ET auction, Fed speakers, AMC earnings tonight (held names and peers) with expected move vs cushion, levels for the last 2 hours, if-then lines.

**Section 3 — Action plan:** cancel list first, then afternoon orders (feed-row column filled), do-not-do list, gates.

**Appendix:** A1 book tables, fills and working-order audit · A2 index matrix, sector table, Mag 7, vol/gamma · A5 sources + Integrity · A6 Handoff (ACTIONS = afternoon orders + PRE actions not done).

## Mode rules
- Every market number is "since 09:30 ET".
- A section with nothing new since PRE gets one line: `No change since PM-<date> — <key fact>`.
- If the MID feed is missing, Section 1 opens with `STALE FEED (<age>)`, morning execution is `UNVERIFIED`, and no position may be added beyond the last feed's table.

## Common mistakes
- Re-reporting pre-market news as new.
- Quoting VWAP distance, volume pacing or 0DTE flow without a live source (print N/A).
- Calling a lunch-hour drift a reversal without a level.
- Copying the morning report's positions instead of the feed (the 4 Nov MID copied PRE's invented book).
